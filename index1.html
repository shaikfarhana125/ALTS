DISEASE PREDICTION SYSTEM

Disease prediction is the use of artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques to identify the likelihood of a person developing a disease based on their medical data, such as age, symptoms, lifestyle, and test results. By analyzing large datasets, these models can detect patterns and provide early warnings for conditions like diabetes, heart disease, and cancer. This helps doctors make faster and more accurate decisions, reduces treatment costs, and improves patient outcomes. Overall, disease prediction plays a crucial role in modern healthcare by enabling early diagnosis and preventive care.
